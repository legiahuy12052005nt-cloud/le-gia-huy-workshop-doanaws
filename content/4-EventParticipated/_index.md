&emsp;**Event Name:** Workshop: AWS Architecture, DevOps Culture & Career Journey

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 AM, June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Số 2 đường Hải Triều , phường Bến nghé , Quận 1 , Tp.HCM , Tầng 26

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event shared practical experiences around 4 main topics: Career stories and corporate culture in multinational companies; The real work of a DevOps Engineer (incident handling, CI/CD management, Cloud cost optimization); Analyzing and designing highly scalable system architectures (A scalable URL shortening service on AWS); and an 8-step career development roadmap from a student to an AWS Partner.

&emsp;**Results Achieved:** Helped me clearly understand the practical system operation process on AWS, shifting my mindset from simply "writing code" to "managing and optimizing systems." I acquired scalable architecture design thinking to apply to my internship projects, and learned how to build a professional Portfolio to prepare for future job applications.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AgentForge Building production-ready agentic systems using Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 AM, August 01, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Số 2 đường Hải Triều, phường Bến Nghé, Quận 1, Tp.HCM, Tầng 26

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event explored the progression of Agentic AI and its gradient of autonomy. It provided a deep dive into Amazon Bedrock AgentCore as a comprehensive framework for production, introducing concepts like Model Context Protocol (MCP), Agent2Agent (A2A) communication, and secure serverless runtime. It also outlined a structured 3-day AgentForge roadmap for developing secure, scalable AI systems.

&emsp;**Results Achieved:** Shifted my technical mindset from focusing on single AI models to architecting robust, connected, and observable production systems. I gained a clear, actionable plan to build my portfolio by following the 3-day roadmap: deploying basic agents, integrating external tools via MCP, and applying system optimization and security policies.

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Workshop: AWS Certification Deep Dive (CLF-C02 & AIF-C01)

&emsp;**Time:** 08:00 PM, August 01, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Google Meet (Online)

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event provided a comprehensive breakdown of the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) and AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) exams. It covered core AWS Cloud concepts such as global infrastructure, high availability, and elasticity. It also explored the 6 perspectives of the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF), cost optimization through rightsizing, and the Shared Responsibility Model for security and compliance.

&emsp;**Results Achieved:** Deepened my technical understanding of scalable and fault-tolerant architectural design choices. I acquired a structured, laser-focused roadmap for my exam preparation and gained practical insights into applying "rightsizing" principles to continuously monitor and avoid over-provisioning in my personal AWS lab projects.
