# Event Report: Deep Dive into Agentic AI with Amazon Bedrock

### Event Objectives
- Understand "Agentic AI" and its gradient of autonomy, from simple bots to virtual workers.
- Discover Amazon Bedrock AgentCore as a complete framework for deploying AI agents to production.
- Learn a structured 3-day roadmap for developing secure, serverless agentic systems.

### Guest Speakers
The workshop featured highly experienced AWS product specialists and AI engineering professionals.

### Key Highlights
1. **Agentic AI & Autonomy:** Explored the progression of AI from Simple Assistants to highly autonomous Agentic Virtual Workers.
2. **System Connectivity:** Introduced Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent2Agent (A2A) communication for connecting agents to external tools (Slack, GitHub, Jira) and to each other.
3. **Bedrock AgentCore Framework:** Detailed the core modules needed for production: Context, Tools, Optimization, Environment, and Platform Control.
4. **Serverless Runtime:** Explained the secure AgentCore Runtime and its consumption-based pricing model (e.g., $0.0895/hr per vCPU).
5. **AgentForge 3-Day Roadmap:** A clear guide covering basic deployment (Day 1), memory & observability (Day 2), and DevOps policies (Day 3).

### What I Learned & Application
- **Shift in Mindset:** Transitioned from focusing on single AI models to building robust, connected, and observable production systems.
- **Action Plan:** I will follow the 3-day workshop agenda to build my portfolio—starting with deploying a basic agent, integrating MCP tools, and documenting the process.

> This workshop demystified complex Agentic AI concepts and provided a clear, actionable roadmap for taking AWS agents to production.
