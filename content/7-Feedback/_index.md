> This section reflects my personal insights and hands-on experiences during the 12-week First Cloud AI Journey, aimed at helping the FCAJ team continuously optimize the program for future cohorts.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment** 
The learning and working environment at the AWS Vietnam office is exceptionally dynamic and data-driven. The open-space layout stimulates creativity and provides an ideal setting for interns to engage in high-intensity teamwork, brainstorm system architectures, and optimize complex cloud deployments.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin** 
The mentors (AWS Solutions Architects) possess profound technical expertise, offering dedicated guidance while orienting our mindset toward scalable architectures and cost-optimization. The administrative team is highly supportive and generously provides ample AWS Credits, ensuring a flexible sandbox environment to test data pipelines, train machine learning models, and deploy applications safely.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Major** 
The program directly bridges the gap between university coursework and enterprise-level technology. Instead of focusing solely on local data processing or basic software development, my time at FCAJ deepened my understanding of distributed systems, serverless computing, and integrating intelligent services (like Amazon SageMaker and Bedrock) into real-world applications.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development** 
FCAJ served as a major launchpad for refining my skills in designing optimized cloud architectures and leveraging AI/ML for practical solutions. Furthermore, delivering technical presentations in English significantly elevated my communication and agile teamwork capabilities under tight production deadlines.

**5. Culture & Teamwork** 
Amazon's "Day 1" cultural philosophy is deeply embedded in the program: every intern embraces a strong sense of Ownership and actively tackles new engineering challenges. The cohesion within our cohort, especially the seamless collaboration during group capstone milestones (such as team *ILOVEAWS*), fostered a unified collective ready to troubleshoot night and day.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**
Although the program operates as a training bootcamp, the core value lies in the substantial non-monetary assets provided. The company granted full access to premium global learning paths and extensive resources to build robust sandbox environments. Furthermore, adapting to the professional workplace culture served as a practical lesson, encouraging me to actively invest in my personal branding and global corporate standards.

### Additional Questions

* **What were you most satisfied with during your internship?** 
The invaluable opportunity to design, build, and deploy an end-to-end cloud-native project utilizing world-class AI and infrastructure services. Having the autonomy to iterate on architectures under the direct mentorship of senior industry experts provided deep, practical engineering insights.

* **What do you think the company could improve for future interns?** 
The 12-week bootcamp schedule is highly intensive, packing a vast amount of Cloud and AI knowledge. Extending the final Capstone Project phase by 1–2 weeks would give teams more headroom to fully optimize their source code, train their models more thoroughly, and conduct rigorous load testing.

* **Would you recommend this internship to your peers? Why?** 
Absolutely. This is one of the most practical and high-quality workforce bootcamps in Vietnam for IT students, effectively bridging the gap between university academic theory and global cloud/AI enterprise demands.

* **Experience Enhancement Proposal:** I recommend incorporating specialized deep-dive sessions focusing on MLOps (Machine Learning Operations) and data engineering pipelines to further satisfy students' research passions in applied AI.
